The Story of Hirup Cafe

Founded right here in the heart of Seksyen 9, Shah Alam, Hirup Cafe began with a basic but powerful idea: to take a moment, breathe in, and sip deeply (hirup).

We source local beans alongside premium imports to create an aromatic profile crafted specifically for local tastes. More than just a coffee shop, we wanted to build an engaging digital hub where community connection translates directly to loyalty perks.
