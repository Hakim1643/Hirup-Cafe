The Story of Hirup

Derived from the local word meaning "to sip," Hirup Cafe was built as an escape from the digital rush. We believe that coffee isn't just fuel. It's an environment, a moment of presence, and a celebration of community right here in Shah Alam.

Crafted Environment ✨💫

From our signature dark roasts to our minimalist interior aesthetic, every corner is designed to inspire creators, students, and thinkers alike. Plug in your laptop, grab a pastry, and make yourself at home.
